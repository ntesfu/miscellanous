Setup
1. On this Mac, open Viewer above.
2. On your iPhone (same Wi-Fi), open the Camera URL in Safari and tap through the certificate warning once.
3. Allow camera access. The live feed appears in the Viewer — hit Record.
Recordings save to the recordings/ folder next to server.py.
